2. Derived Business Metrics
------------------------------------------------------------

revenue = price_unit * units_sold

available_supply = stock_available + delivered_qty

sell_through_rate = units_sold / available_supply

fill_rate_raw = delivered_qty / units_sold

inventory_remaining = available_supply - units_sold

stock_utilization = units_sold / available_supply

delivery_status:
- 0–2 days = Healthy
- Above 2–4 days = Watch
- Above 4 days = Critical

promotion_label:
- 1 = Promotion
- 0 = No Promotion
